The back room at Rooney's. Elijah sat on the floor against the wall in a brown turkey sweater his niece had picked out, a bottle two-thirds gone beside him. Ezra sat down next to him.

"They took Renee's kids," Elijah said. "Both of them. Right out of the guest room. I was on the couch. I heard the horn, Ez. I thought it was a truck. I put the pillow over my head."

"I slept through it. Didn't even roll over."

Elijah laughed, and the laugh broke into weeping. "You remember? That stool out there. I said if it was true, wouldn't there be a sign."

"And I said my daughter drew one."

"There were signs every night on that TV. Kambria sat on that stool and told me to my face, and I made a joke and bought her a ginger ale. I'm the guy who makes the joke. That's all I ever was. Where'd they go, Ez? Where'd they all go?"

"They didn't die, Eli. They were taken. Jesus said He'd come back for His own. The dead in Christ first, then the living, caught up together. In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye. That sound you heard was the trump of God."

"And us?"

"We were asleep. Both of us. But Angela wrote it down, every page. The door's still open a little while. Whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be delivered."

"Whoever?"

"Whoever."

"Even a drunk on a bar floor in a turkey sweater?"

"Whoever. I read it twice last night to make sure."

"All of it? You believe all of it?"

"All of it. It's not too late. That's the part she underlined."
