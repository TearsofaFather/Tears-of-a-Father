Privacy

Tears of a Father, the website and the Android app · Updated November 13, 2026

The app opens tearsofafather.com. The website and the app work the same way, and this page covers both.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell, share or receive any information about who you are or what you read.

What stays on your device

A few settings are kept in your own browser or app storage so the site remembers them: your text size, the last chapter you opened, the Bible passage you were on, and the notes you write in the Notebook. They never leave your device unless you turn on Notebook sync, and you can clear them at any time by clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data.

Notebook sync (optional)

If you choose to sync your Notebook between devices, you pick a sync phrase. Your notes are encrypted on your device with that phrase before anything is sent. Our server stores only the encrypted copy, filed under a one-way code made from the phrase. We cannot read your notes, and we cannot recover them if you lose the phrase. If you never turn on sync, nothing is sent.

Audio and pages

Chapters, audio and images are delivered from our web host, Cloudflare. Like any web host, it handles your device's network address in order to deliver the pages and may keep short-lived technical logs for security. We do not use those logs to identify readers.

Children

The site and app collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent to the contact address on the app's Google Play listing.
